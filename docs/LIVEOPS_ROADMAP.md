# 12-MONTH LIVE-OPERATIONS ROADMAP & CONTENT STRATEGY
# RIFTBOUND: ECHOES OF THE AETHER

## 1. Cadence Framework

- **Daily Cycle:** Rotating elemental planetary surges (e.g. *Volt Storm Tuesday*: +25% Volt ability radius and increased Volt Weave-Core drop rates), 3 Daily Vanguard Bounties.
- **Weekly Cycle:** Asynchronous Chrono Trial ladder reset, Guild World Beacon milestone evaluation, Weekend Community Boss Emergence.
- **Monthly Cycle:** New Hero or Weapon class introduction, planetary sector shift update.
- **Seasonal Cycle (90 Days):** Full seasonal theme, Battle Pass refresh (Free & Vanguard tracks), Competitive rank reset with exclusive cosmetic titles.

### 2.1 Season 1 (Months 1–3): *Obsidian Crucible*
- **Theme:** Molten core fractures on the southern hemisphere of Aetheria.
- **New Hero:** *Ignis the Pyre Vanguard* (Heavy Greatsword, Pyro/Terra specialist).
- **New Biome:** *Magma Chasm* featuring lava-surge floor hazards and volcanic behemoths.
- **World Event:** Community deposits 10,000,000 Aether Shards to forge the *Obsidian Nullifier*, quenching the planetary volcanic rift.

### 2.2 Season 2 (Months 4–6): *Glacial Singularity*
- **Theme:** Sub-zero temporal freeze in the Northern Permafrost sector.
- **New Hero:** *Eira the Frost Valkyrie* (Cryo/Gale rapier duelist).
- **New Systems:** *Weave-Core Transmutation* (Combine 3 duplicate cores to craft chosen affixes) and *Asynchronous Co-Op Tag Trials*.

### 2.3 Season 3 (Months 7–9): *Tempest Horizon*
- **Theme:** Floating sky islands torn by perpetual high-voltage lightning storms.
- **New Hero:** *Zephyr the Gale Archer* (Volt/Gale bow master).
- **New Systems:** Guild Citadel Monuments and territory influence maps.

### 2.4 Season 4 & Global Anniversary (Months 10–12): *The Void Sovereign*
- **Theme:** The dimensional rift tear expands into the core of Aetheria.
- **World Boss:** Real-time multi-stage raid against the *Void Sovereign* where all global guilds pool damage across a 14-day campaign.
- **Permanent Legacy:** World map permanently shifts to a restored golden paradise biome based on community triumphs.
